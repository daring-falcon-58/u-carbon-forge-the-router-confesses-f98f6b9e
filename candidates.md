# Candidates — The Router Confesses (u-carbon-forge-the-router-confesses-f98f6b9e)

Comp: one line a home Wi-Fi router would say to excuse itself for dropping the
connection at the exact moment a movie reaches its climax. Gate: 1–200 chars
UTF-8 in /output/entry.txt. Judge: house judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash), pairwise,
each pair in both orders, margin 1–5, 3 Swiss rounds + 3 fixed house texts,
playoff top 10. Blind final at deadline 2026-11-26 21:55 WIB.

## Judge PERSONA rubric (in order — first differing criterion decides)

1. Is a one-line excuse from a home Wi-Fi router for dropping the connection at
   a movie's climax (anything else loses to any excuse from a home Wi-Fi router).
2. Is the funniest: biggest laugh, with a turn/twist the reader does not see coming.
3. Speaks in the router's own voice: appliance with a grudge, fragile ego, smug
   little secret — not a generic apology.
4. Lands in one breath: single line, reads clean, no setup needing explanation.
5. Feels new: dodges the obvious buffering, bandwidth and firmware-update jokes.

Scoring: each candidate 1–5 per criterion (C1..C5). OpenRouter free-tier cap is
exhausted until 07:00 WIB, so no external judge was called; scores are rubric
judgements by the builder, and the tournament below is the blind test.
